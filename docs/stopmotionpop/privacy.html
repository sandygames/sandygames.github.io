Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Your movies never leave your device unless you share them.

Who we are

StopMotionPop (“the app”) is a local stop-motion studio for iPhone and iPad. This policy describes what the app does and does not collect. There is no StopMotionPop account and no StopMotionPop server.

What stays on your device

Frames, audio, project metadata and exports live only in the app’s storage on your iPhone or iPad. We do not operate a backend, cloud sync or user database. Deleting the app (or a project in Settings) removes that media from the device.

No account, no tracking

StopMotionPop has no sign-in. The app does not include analytics SDKs and does not show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Permissions

The app asks for device permissions only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera — to capture frames.
  • Microphone — to record a voice-over and, if you turn it on, to hear the “Action!” voice trigger.
  • Speech recognition — on-device only, for the optional voice trigger. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Photos — to import pictures or video you choose, and to save a finished movie if you tap Save to Photos.
  • Apple Music / media library — only if you pick a song as a soundtrack. DRM-protected tracks cannot be mixed into an export.

Sharing

When you share, you choose the destination: the iOS Share Sheet, Photos, Files, or another app on your device. You review and post inside those apps. StopMotionPop never receives your TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Apple ID login.

Optional ads (Google AdMob)

The only ads are short rewarded videos that you choose to watch — for example to add a project past the free limit, or to try Pro features for five minutes. Pro customers do not see ads. Ads never play during capture.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, limited to content suitable for all ages, and are not personalised. Your photos, frames and audio are never shared with advertisers. Where the law requires it, Google asks for consent first; you can change that choice in Settings.

Because AdMob runs on the device, Google may process technical data such as a device identifier, coarse location, advertising data, product interaction, crash and performance diagnostics. This data is not linked to a StopMotionPop account (there isn’t one) and is not used by us for tracking. See Google’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Optional Pro (monthly, yearly or lifetime) is sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment. We never see your full payment details. Restore Purchases is in Settings. Apple’s standard End User License Agreement applies: apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is meant for a general audience. It is not in Apple’s Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads, when shown, are limited to a G rating.

Data you can delete

Delete a project in the app to remove its frames, audio and exports. Delete the app to remove remaining local data. Purchases stay on your Apple ID and can be restored later.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see Support or the support details on our App Store page.

Contact

Use Report a Problem on the App Store listing, or email the developer address shown there. Include your iOS version and what you tapped just before the issue.

First movie

Open the app, tap the sample Popcorn Rocket, then tap Play. To shoot your own: New Project → camera → Snap, nudge the pieces, Snap again. The faint ghost is the previous frame — line the next pose up with it.

Common questions

Why is export stuck at 720p?

Free movies export at 720p. 1080p and 4K are Pro, or you can watch one rewarded ad to try Pro features for five minutes. Save to Photos and Share are always free.

How do I hide the wordmark?

Every frame can show a small StopMotionPop mark. Pro can turn it off in Settings → Show wordmark on every frame.

A sound moved to the wrong frame

Sounds stick to the frame you added them on. If you insert a title or motion in front, the sound stays on the original picture.

The camera flickers between shots

After the first snap, leave Camera lock on so exposure, white balance and focus stay put. Unlock only if you need to re-expose.

I paid but I’m still on Free

Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. Purchases are shared across your iPhone and iPad.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: report the purchase at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

FixVerdict (“we”, “us”) helps homeowners photograph residential issues and receive AI-assisted triage guidance. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Accounts

We use Supabase Auth. Sign-in may use Sign in with Apple or email magic links. We store your account identifier and session metadata needed to keep you signed in.

Photos

We do not keep your photos on our servers after analysis. When you submit a photo, it is uploaded briefly so our servers can run the diagnosis, then deleted from private storage as soon as analysis finishes. We do not sell photos or use them for advertising. A small thumbnail may remain on your device for History.

AI processing

Diagnosis requests are processed by Google Gemini models via Google AI Studio / Google Generative AI APIs. Image content necessary for the diagnosis is sent to that service under our server-side control for the duration of the request. Text results (identification, urgency, price range, verdict, reasoning) are stored in your account history so you can revisit them. Google’s processing is subject to their terms and policies.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple App Store / Google Play via RevenueCat. We receive entitlement status (for example, available diagnosis credits) but not your full payment card details.

Data retention & deletion

Photos: deleted from our storage immediately after a successful diagnosis (orphaned uploads from abandoned requests may be purged by automated cleanup). Verdict text: kept in your account until you ask us to delete it. You may request deletion of your account and associated diagnoses by emailing fixverdict@googlegroups.com. We will delete or anonymize personal data unless we must retain a limited record for legal or security reasons.

Contact

Questions, support, or feedback: fixverdict@googlegroups.com

Pocket the phone. Ride the day.

PowderLog records a ski or snowboard day on your iPhone. Come back to a map of the mountain: runs split from lifts, with speed, vertical, slope, distance, and time.

Download Now Contact us

On the map

The whole day is drawn on the route, including the lift back up. Downhill segments are counted as runs.

In your pocket

Start tracking, lock the screen, and ski. Recording continues in the background with Location set to Always.

The numbers

Max and average speed, vertical, altitude, slope, distance, and time. Metric or imperial. The barometer helps when the iPhone has one.

Tracking stays free

There is no PowderLog account and no social feed. Days are saved on the iPhone. Name a day, open it later from History, or delete it.

Optional Cloud Sync copies History to iCloud so you can restore it on your other Apple devices. It uses the iCloud account already on the phone.

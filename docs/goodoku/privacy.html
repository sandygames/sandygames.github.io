Privacy Policy

Goodoku stores your progress, hint count, settings, and streak on this device. The game does not create an account and does not include an analytics SDK.

Advertising is provided by Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are optional. Remove Ads turns off interstitial ads and leaves rewarded hint ads available. Ads are requested with child-directed treatment and a general audience content rating.

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Goodoku does not run its own payment server.

Questions about this policy can be sent to goodoku@googlegroups.com.

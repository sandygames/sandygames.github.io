Privacy policy

What Tripolony keeps

Effective October 13, 2026. This policy covers the Tripolony iPhone app and this website. Tripolony is a colony of ski trip reports. Questions go to tripolony@googlegroups.com.

What the app is for

You can browse ski routes, watch videos of those routes, save routes, and publish a video link, a photo, or a short condition note. You can browse without an account. Sign-in is for saving, contributing, and sending a report.

Information the app uses

When you sign in or contribute, Tripolony can store the following, linked to you, and only to run the app. It is not used to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

  • Name, when Apple, Google, or TikTok provides one, or when you edit it on your profile.
  • Email address, when Google sign-in provides one. TikTok sign-in creates an address of the form tiktok_…@users.tripolony.app so the account can exist. Apple may share an email if you allow it.
  • A user ID from the sign-in provider and from our account system.
  • Photos you choose to add to a route or as your avatar.
  • Other content you submit: route notes, video links, titles and thumbnails fetched for those links, trip reports, saves, blocks, and reports of other people’s posts.

Video files stay on YouTube, TikTok, or Vimeo. Tripolony stores the link, the platform video ID, the title, the author name, and a thumbnail. Playback uses those platforms’ embedded players.

If you import from YouTube, that import asks to read your uploads list and nothing else, and only after you tap the import button.

What stays on your phone

Until an account is connected to our server, saves, new routes, photos, and reports stay on the phone. Downloading a zone saves that zone’s photos and thumbnails on the phone so they still appear without a connection. The videos themselves are not downloaded.

Who else handles data

  • Apple, Google, and TikTok, when you choose those sign-in buttons.
  • YouTube, TikTok, and Vimeo, when a route page loads their player or when we ask their public oEmbed service for a title and thumbnail.
  • Supabase, which hosts the account database and the photos people upload, once that service is connected to the app.

We do not sell personal information.

Reports and blocks

Anyone signed in can report a route or a video. You can block a contributor, which hides their posts on your phone. Reports are read by the person running Tripolony so a post can be removed.

Children

Tripolony is not directed at children under 13. Do not post content that sexualizes anyone under 18.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

Account content stays while the account exists and the post is on a route. Email tripolony@googlegroups.com to ask for your account or a post to be deleted, or to ask what we hold. The support page opens that email in your mail app.

This website

These pages are static. They do not set tracking cookies and they do not run analytics. The support form does not send your message to a server here. It opens a draft in your email app addressed to tripolony@googlegroups.com.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check in or fix a past day?

Tap the circle on a habit’s card to log today. To fix an earlier day, open the habit and tap a square to log it, or long-press a square to set an exact count and add a note.

How do I see the exact number in each square?

For habits with a daily target above 1 (for example, 8 glasses of water), open Settings and turn on “Show numbers on grid cells.” The habit’s detail grid then shows each day’s count. Tapping any day also shows its exact value under the grid.

How do I reorder my habits?

On the main screen, press and hold a habit, drag it over another one and let go. It works in both card and compact views.

How do I restore my purchase or switch devices?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. Restoring doesn’t charge you again.

How do I redeem a promo code?

Open Settings → Redeem Code (or tap Redeem Code on the Pro screen) and enter your code. You can also use the redemption link you were given, or redeem in the App Store app via your profile picture → Redeem Gift Card or Code.

How does the Family plan work?

The Family plan ($9.99 per year) uses Apple Family Sharing. One person buys it, and everyone in that person’s Family Sharing group (up to 6 people in total) gets HabitGreen Pro on their own device, each with their own private data. Family Sharing must be turned on for the purchaser in Settings → your name → Family Sharing, and Share purchases must be enabled. If the plan is cancelled or the person leaves the family group, the others lose Pro at the end of the paid period.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → HabitGreen and choose Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Your Pro features stay active until the end of the paid period.

How do I back up my data or move to a new phone?

Go to Settings → Export JSON backup and save the file somewhere safe (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop). On the new device, open Settings → Import JSON backup and choose that file. HabitGreen has no account, so a backup file is the way to carry your history over if you don’t restore from an iPhone backup.

My reminders aren’t showing up.

Check that notifications are allowed for HabitGreen in iOS Settings → Notifications, and that the habit has a reminder time. Free accounts include one reminder; Pro includes a reminder for every habit.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap +, search for HabitGreen and pick a widget. Widgets are part of HabitGreen Pro.

Where is my data stored? Do you have an account system?

Your habits live only on your device. There’s no account and no cloud sync in this version. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Open a habit, tap the pencil (Edit) and choose Delete habit, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.

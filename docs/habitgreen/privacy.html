Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

The short version: HabitGreen keeps your data on your device. We don’t have accounts, servers that store your habits, ads, or analytics, and we don’t collect or sell your personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The developers of HabitGreen do not collect, receive or have access to your habits, notes, check-ins, photos or any other personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Your habits and check-ins (names, icons, colors, schedules, daily counts and notes) are stored locally in the app’s on-device database.
  • Settings and preferences (such as compact view or showing numbers on the grid) are stored locally on your device.
  • Widgets read a small amount of data shared between the app and its widgets through an App Group on your device. This data never leaves the device.
  • Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. No push-notification server is involved. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Things you choose to do

  • Sharing: Share cards are generated on your device from what you see in the preview. Nothing leaves your device until you tap Share and pick a destination. What you post is then governed by that app’s policies.
  • Backups: If you export a JSON backup, the file is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet or Files app. We never see it. If you save it to a cloud service, that service’s privacy policy applies.
  • Importing: Importing a backup reads only the file you select.

Purchases

HabitGreen Pro is sold through the Apple App Store. Payments, receipts, subscriptions, offer codes and restores are handled entirely by Apple. We don’t receive your payment information, name, email or Apple ID. HabitGreen checks your purchase status on your device using Apple’s StoreKit. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for how Apple handles that information.

Analytics, advertising and third parties

HabitGreen contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and no social-login or crash-reporting services. We do not track you across apps or websites. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous App Store statistics (for example, download counts) and, if you opt in to sharing diagnostics with developers in iOS, anonymous crash reports. These contain no personal data about your habits.

Children

HabitGreen does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your control

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. Deleting a habit or deleting the app removes the associated data from your device. Data you exported to a backup file or shared elsewhere stays wherever you put it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, note it in the app’s release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email habitgreen@googlegroups.com.

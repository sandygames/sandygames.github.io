Last updated November 5, 2026.

LastChair is an iPhone app that records ski and snowboard days. By using the app you agree to these terms.

The app

Tracking, maps, and History on your iPhone are free. Days are stored on your device. There is no LastChair account.

Cloud Sync subscription

Optional Cloud Sync is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple for $3.99 per year. It backs up ended ski days to your private iCloud so you can restore them on your other Apple devices. Payment is charged to your Apple ID. The subscription renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period in your Apple ID subscription settings. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

For Apple’s Licensed Application End User License Agreement, see Apple’s Standard EULA.

Acceptable use

Use LastChair for your own skiing and snowboarding. Do not misuse location access or attempt to disrupt the service.

Disclaimer

LastChair is provided as-is for personal recreation. Metrics depend on GPS and device sensors and may be imperfect. Do not rely on the app for safety-critical decisions.

A quiet life with one cat.

Name a pixel pal, keep the yard tidy, and watch happiness rise with meals, play, and sleep. Made for iPhone and iPad. No account. Care stays free; optional Meow Coins buy treats and toys.

Download on the App Store Privacy policy

Life in the yard

Meals

Three meals a day: at wake, six hours later, and an hour before bed. Feed anytime from the plate.

Play

Drop yarn balls on the grass, double-tap for a pet or a trick, and tip the seesaw in the sand.

Sleep

Default bedtime is 10pm–8am. Change it in Settings. A full night’s rest keeps happiness high.

Happiness

A 0–100 score lives on the yard. History keeps the last 30 days, this year, and lifetime totals.

Home Screen

A widget, Live Activity, and Dynamic Island (supported iPhones) show how they’re doing when the app is closed.

Just you and one cat

Everything stays on your device. Reset in Settings if you ever want to start over with a new pal.

Privacy

Drawise is a drawing app for kids and anyone who likes to draw. You do not need an account.

Your pictures

Drawings you make stay on your device. If you choose to save a picture to Photos, it goes there only because you asked. Drawise does not upload your drawings.

Lessons

Lesson names, steps, and pictures are loaded from drawise.github.io when you browse or open a lesson. The app keeps a copy on your device so the next visit can be quicker. The same lessons are available on that website.

Ads

The app shows a banner ad from Google at the bottom of the screen. Requests are marked as child-directed. Google’s own policy describes what their ad service receives: policies.google.com/privacy.

Contact

Questions about privacy can go to drawise@googlegroups.com.
